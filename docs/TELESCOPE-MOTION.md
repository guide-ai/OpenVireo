## Manual steps

1. Open `http://127.0.0.1:5173/#observatory`, open the dome and locate **Move the telescope**. Current RA/Dec are repeated beside the controls. Hold **North/East/South/West** with pointer, Space or Enter; release to stop. Change **Manual motion multiplier** while stopped and compare finder/telescope views.
2. Select a catalog target and choose **Slew to selected object**. Cancel first and verify unchanged pointing; then confirm. Alternatively edit **Enter coordinates** in the target card and choose **Slew to coordinate draft**. The immediate Point controls remain separate shortcuts.
3. During a sufficiently long slew, choose **Stop telescope motion**. The current committed position stays; the clock can continue. Pause the observatory clock, change view, navigate away or hide the tab to exercise the other release paths.
4. Save during motion, reload and restore. The new22 file restores the stopped pointing/clock and manual multiplier; an existing spectrum/notebook keeps its counts, RNG and pupil values. Malformed22 files keep the prior investigation.
5. Enter RA25 or Dec86 and select **Slew to coordinate draft**: a local error appears without confirmation and a running clock continues. For the retained precision limit, point to RA0.5/Dec0, use finder/default4×, hold North for40 callbacks (800ms), then slew back to the0.5/0 draft. The motion guard may withhold that return and pause the clock; it must not show a false clock-limit alert. The separate **Point to coordinates** shortcut remains available. Returning to an already verified observing configuration still requires explicit activation; no moving-source counts are created.
