## Original contract and numerical policy

Original executable SHA-256: `781bc9704b4674038267834a9ed21d502c455378d8fc9eb15a1653e20c52c54c`. [Provenance](../data/observatory/site-clock-provenance.json) pins the binary/license hashes, raw Single/Extended constants with addresses and exact decimal values, six disassembly ranges and the independent reference script. [Reference scenarios](../data/observatory/site-clock-vectors.json) are generated mathematical checks, not original observations. Raw recovery stays outside source at `/home/dobleuber/Documents/Codex/2026-10-18/task-3/observatory-clock-recovery/`.

- **SC-C01, `0x4881ac..0x488205`:** `d=JD−2451545`, `T=d/36525`, `theta=T²(0.000387933−T·2.583311806e−8)+360.98564736629·d+280.46061837`. The coefficient spellings above summarize the original constants; their exact raw Extended values are pinned in the packet.
- **SC-C02, `0x488208..0x488274`, RTL `0x40365c..0x403686`:** fractional turns use truncation toward zero, then multiply by 360 and the original radians-per-degree Extended. Negative radians receive one original full-circle addition. The RTL temporarily selects truncation in the x87 control word and restores it.
- **SC-C03, `0x488274..0x4882eb`:** add selected east-positive longitude in radians, multiply by 24 and divide by the original full-circle constant; add 24 below zero, subtract 24 only strictly above 24. Do not silently replace that strict comparison with a generic modulo.
- **SC-C04, consumer `0x5384d6..0x53852e`:** the native clock rebuilds its date from host time plus an environment offset and passes Environment+`0x2b86` to the sidereal procedure. Existing optical recovery `0x53df24..0x53e177` maps the selected site's stored longitude into that field. The browser uses the pinned original radians, not a rounded degree display or longitude-sign guess.
- **SC-C05, `0x487bcc..0x487be7` and `0x58b2ec..0x58b441`:** native Julian date and its modal date/offset update are separate from sidereal arithmetic; only modal result1 commits its offset. That host/local-date pipeline and clock callback ordering are not ported here. The explicit Gregorian UT text parser, UTC-to-JD conversion and Apply/Cancel worksheet are new browser adapters.

Production arithmetic is JavaScript Number, including projection of raw Extended constants to binary64. The independent reference uses Decimal100, raw native constants, an integer Gregorian ordinal and exact seconds/day. All21 scenarios agree within `1e−8 h` (36 microseconds); display rounds to eight decimal hours. That tolerance is a bounded mathematical implementation check, not a bound on native runtime or astronomical accuracy. The input range and whole-second rule avoid the unresolved native historical-calendar, host-time, leap-second and DST branches. No external astronomy library or substituted current-era sidereal formula was used.
