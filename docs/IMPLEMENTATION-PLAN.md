## Product and scope

OpenCLEA is a browser reconstruction of CLEA's educational observatories, instruments, data and exercise workflows. The opening experience must be an observatory: open the dome, find an object, point the telescope and reach the appropriate instrument. An unfamiliar learner must be able to explore a first object without entering redshift step sizes, coordinates or instructor preferences.

English is the initial product language. UI/help text lives behind stable semantic keys in `src/i18n/en.json`; presentation formatting is locale-aware. Additional translations and a language selector are deferred. Scientific inputs, exports, identifiers, archival labels, units and numerical policy remain explicitly specified independently of display locale.

The baseline is **58 functional capability packages**, including **15 historical CLEA exercise paths and 2 associated application/exercise paths**. Six additional quality/release workstreams are tracked separately, giving **64 work packages**. Their effort is explicitly included, not added again, in the total below. A capability package is a coherent testable outcome, not a button, function, executable or screen. Shared engines are implemented once; exercise-specific content and edition differences still require their own acceptance scenarios. These are planning counts for the scope below, not a verified claim that the old software contains exactly 58 features. Discovery may split or merge packages. Plan for roughly **55–75 functional packages** until the remaining editions are inventoried.

VIREO is the platform integrating several exercises; it is not counted again as a sixteenth CLEA exercise. Jupiter and Roemer share a package but remain two exercise paths. HR uses VIREO rather than a separately established executable. Astrometry Toolkit and Height of Lunar Mountains are associated scope, with their own versions and permissions; the latter is not represented as one of the 15 CLEA exercises.

## Acceptance and equivalence strategy

Use three separate claims:

- **Representation recovered:** original bytes, formats, instructions and static flow have traceable evidence.
- **Numerical equivalence assessed:** same inputs/state and an explicitly chosen precision/rounding policy meet a routine-specific comparison criterion.
- **Functional fidelity assessed:** the same action sequence/options/time/data produce matching observable behavior, including errors and cancellation.

A green unit test, plausible sky or self-consistent mathematical model proves none of the latter two by itself. Do not use a universal epsilon or method-count percentage. Required evidence per scientific contract: artifact hash, address/body ranges, constants, ABI/stack/FPU inputs/return, conversions, source fields, units, side effects, failure paths, independent cases and remaining uncertainty.

Before a dynamic oracle, prepare a concrete authorized environment and scenarios; execution/emulation of original CLEA remains outside current permission. Static arithmetic reconstruction can proceed now. Later oracle runs should record edition/hash/options/catalogs, date/site, x87 control word where relevant, seed/state and timing. Compare values before/after Extended→double/float32 storage, bit patterns when meaningful, displayed text separately, and generated file records. Repair decompiler bodies/prototypes only in temporary analysis databases.

For stochastic output, first recover PRNG/seed/state and draw ordering. Compare deterministic seeded sequences if available. Otherwise predefine observable effect sizes and appropriate uncertainty/power; failing to reject KS/chi-square is not proof of equivalence. Browser timers must not silently become a new physical acquisition model.

Test families: original-format records and round trips; independent arithmetic/physical checks; RA wrap/poles/field orientation; narrow spectral features; threshold/overflow/underflow/signed-zero/quantization cases; complete action/cancellation/reentry paths; malformed/untrusted imports; student and instructor scenarios; accessibility/mobile; catalog loading/performance; durable-save migrations when added. Keep synthetic vectors distinctly identified as test vectors.
