## Provenance and remaining limits

The v3 analysis archive now accepts distinct strict star and galaxy journals. Current observations and measurement origins must match the archived kind, ID, derivative SHA and filename. Restoration replays counters and regenerates the derivative. Changed domains/policies, cached counts, wrong-kind links, stripped known provenance and altered journal IDs fail atomically. Mixed archives retain the existing8-snapshot/24000-event bounds and legacy v1/v2 compatibility. A normalized external curve alone does not certify acquisition.

Galaxy measurement origins enter velocity/Hubble using only the saved manual z. Their VIREO target identification cannot be changed to another target. Magnitudes and their sources remain explicit operator inputs; the original catalog value is documented above. Stellar origins remain excluded even after relabeling or mixed-session restore. One galaxy cannot establish the multi-galaxy origin fit or assess scatter, target selection or cosmological-model accuracy.

Unmeasured native GDI sampling, palette/device behavior and original runtime trajectories remain unknown. Other galaxies, pointings, telescope settings, photo draws, zero-axis branches and arbitrary intervals are not enabled. The fallback spectrum is a shifted common template, not a newly recovered object-specific observation. Scientific source data remain licensed material; no public redistribution is authorized.

No Claude, Windows, original execution/emulation, installation, public upload or push was used. The current target-free Java probe reports runtime_missing where the earlier configured research environment completed analyses; that difference did not prevent reuse of the immutable saved disassembly and original data. No repair/reinstall was attempted. External reviews remain pending under the existing restriction.
