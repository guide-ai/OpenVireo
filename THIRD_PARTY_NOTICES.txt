Camera catalog data from CDS/VizieR (27 October 2026)
The CCD camera reads NOMAD (VizieR I/297) and USNO-B1.0 (VizieR I/284), and the IR camera reads only NOMAD, through CDS/VizieR, as bundled extracts or a live cone search. Recorded test responses (tests/fixtures/vizier) and bounded extracts (public/camera/vizier, see data/camera/EXTRACTS-PROVENANCE.json) are included under the user decision of 27 October 2026. Please cite: CDS/VizieR, doi:10.26093/cds/vizier (Ochsenbein, Bauer and Marcout 2000, A&AS 143, 23); NOMAD, Zacharias N. et al. 2004, AAS 205, 48.15; USNO-B1.0, Monet D.G. et al. 2003, AJ 125, 984. VizieR declares no explicit licence for these two catalogs; they are probably US-government USNO works, which is recorded as uncertain. They are not CLEA resources, and the images built from them are simulations, not observations.
The IR camera uses the NOMAD J, H and K magnitudes, which come from the Two Micron All Sky Survey. This work uses data products from 2MASS (Skrutskie M.F. et al. 2006, AJ 131, 1163), a joint project of the University of Massachusetts and the Infrared Processing and Analysis Center/California Institute of Technology, funded by NASA and the National Science Foundation.
