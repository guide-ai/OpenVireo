**Load Spectrum for Measurement**
To load a spectrum for measurement, select "File"->"Data"->"Load Saved
Spectrum..." from the measuring tool menu, and select a spectrum file to
be loaded.

Related Topics:
    "Save Spectrum for Analysis" on the spectrometer "Help" menu.

**Measure Individual Spectral Features**
You can measure the position and relative intensity of individual
spectral features on a displayed spectrum.  Press and hold the left
mouse button as you position the cross-hair cursor on the feature you
wish to measure.  The position of the cursor is displayed in readouts at
the bottom of the screen in converted form as wavelength and relative
intensity.  When you release the mouse button a vertical line appears on
the screen at the wavelength position, and a short horizontal line
appears at the intensity level.  You can adjust the position of these
lines using the numeric keypad arrow keys to improve your measurements
before recording them. You can also move the lines to the next feature
you wish to measure by pressing and holding an arrow key (see below).

    -Keyboard Access-
    The arrow keys on the numeric keypad (keys "2","4","6","8") may be
    use to position the measuring lines in place of the mouse. NumLock
    must be "On" and MouseKeys must be "Off".  (If you are using
    MouseKeys, configure your system so that MouseKeys are activated
    when the NumLock is "Off".)  If the measuring lines are not visible,
    press the right arrow key ("6"), and the lines will appear from the
    left of the display.

**Load Comparison Spectrum**
To load a comparison spectrum, select "Comparison Spectrum"->"Select"
from the measuring tool menu, and pick a set of lines from the list that
appears.  The comparison lines will appear on the display and the slider
control will be enabled.  You may load as many sets of lines as you
like.  All selected lines will be displayed until the comparison
spectrum is cleared.

See also;
    Measure With Comparison Spectrum
    Identify Comparison Lines
    Clear Comparison Spectrum

**Measure With Comparison Spectrum**
You can measure the doppler shift of a spectrum against a comparison
spectrum of many lines.  Simply load a comparison spectum (note that
more than one set of lines may be loaded) and use the slider to move the
comparison until a match is made the spectral lines of the object
spectrum.  The Z value corresponding to the shift of the comparison
spectrum is displayed below the slider.  Note the following:

    1)  You may not get an exact fit on every line.  You will have to
        make a judgement call as to the best overall fit.
 
    2)  Your measurements will be more accurate if you zoom the
        spectrum.  This may however make it impossible to see all the
        lines at the same time.  (See Help topics listed below.)

    3)  You may feel that it is more accurate to measure the lines
        individually.  (See Help topic "Measure Individual Spectral
        Features".)  You can use the comparison spectrum to identify the
        lines, but you will have to record your results and compute an
        average Z offline.  Whatever value appears in the Z read-out
        will not be valid.

**Identify Comparison Lines**
To identify a line in the comparison spectrum, place the mouse cursor
near the line on the display and press the right mouse button.  The box
that appears will show the line ID, the rest and shifted wavelengths,
and the line source category.

See also;
    Load Comparison Spectrum
    Measure With Comparison Spectrum

**Clear Comparison Spectrum**
To clear the comparison spectrum from the display, select "Comparison
Spectrum"->"Clear" from the measuring tool menu.  All comparison lines
will be removed from the display.

**Record Measurements**
To record the Z value that you have measured, select "File"->"Data"->
"Record Measurements..."  If you have measured with the comparison
spectrum slider, the Z value from the readout will already be entered on
the form.  If you are measuring the lines idividually, the entered value
will not be correct and you will have to replace it with your computed
average value.

When you press "OK" the information you entered is placed on a list, but
is not saved to a file.  To review, print, and/or save your results to a
file, select "Tools"->"Results Editors"-> "Observational Results"->
"Display/Print/Save..." on the observatory control panel menu.

See also;
    Measure With Comparison Spectrum
    Measure Individual Spectral Features
    "Results Editors" on the observatory control panel "Help" menu.

**Zoom/Scan Spectrum**
To Zoom a spectrum you are measuring, press the "Zoom" button.  A box
with sliders will appear that allows you to set the wavelength range
displayed in the measuring window.  The sliders change the wavelengths
in 100 Angstrom steps.  The "Reset" button returns the display to the
original (full) wavelength range.

When a display is zoomed, the scan arrow keys below the zoom button are
enabled.  Use them to change the visible portion of the spectrum.  Like
the sliders, they operate in 100 Angstrom steps.

Zooming the spectrum allows a more accurate determination of Z, since
the finer pixelization yields a smaller stepsize in the placement of the
measuring line and/or comparison spectrum.  The downside when measuring
with a comparison spectrum is that not all the lines may be visible at
the same time to deterimine the best overall fit.

**Set Slider Sensitivity**
The "Fine"/"Coarse" switch above the comparison spectrum slider controls
the slider sensitivity.  Use the "Coarse" setting only when you need to
move the comparison spectrum a long distance.

Related Topics:
    Re-center the Slider
    Measure With Comparison Spectrum

**Re-center the Slider**
If you reach the end of the comparsion spectrum slider and need to move
the comparison spectrum further, press the small button to the right of
the Z read-out to re-center the slider.

Related Topics:
    Set Slider Sensitivity
    Measure With Comparison Spectrum

**Close Measurement Window**
To close the measurement tool window and return to the observatory
control panel, select "File"->"Exit Spectrum Measuring" on the menu, or
press the "X" box in the upper right corner.
